# ACE opportunity — what to enter

The hackathon asks for an **ACE Opportunity ID**: an alphanumeric id that AWS
Partner Central generates the moment you create an opportunity in ACE Pipeline
Manager. You do **not** need AWS to approve or validate it first — created is
enough — so this is a form-filling task, not a waiting one.

What you cannot skip: **the marketing campaign code**. Without it the
opportunity is not tagged to this hackathon.

```
AWS CDS Agentic AI Hackathon -Sept. 2026
```

It sits under **Opportunity marketing details → Sourced from marketing activity
→ Marketing campaign**. Copy it exactly, including the spacing around the dash.

## Before you can fill anything in

You need access to **ACE Pipeline Manager** in Partner Central. Access is granted
per user by your organisation's **Alliance Lead** — at Innominds that is whoever
administers the AWS partnership. If `partnercentral.awspartner.com` does not show
you an ACE tab, that person is the one to contact, and they are the only
blocker here that you cannot solve yourself.

### Project description

> ReLaunch India helps employers and employees across industries do their work
> effectively with AI, demonstrate the improvement with reviewed evidence, and
> apply that capability to current responsibilities or to suitable
> opportunities. An administrator configures a role: its recurring tasks,
> permitted AI tools, reference material and assessment criteria. AI drafts the
> rubric and task set; a domain expert must approve and calibrate it before any
> result counts as a validated assessment.
>
> A worker submits a real work sample. Automated checks run on observable
> outcomes, an approved rubric is applied with every finding tied to evidence in
> the artifact, and a human reviewer approves the conclusions. The worker is
> taught the AI-assisted workflow, practises the observed gap, and is reassessed
> on an unseen task of comparable difficulty. Only reviewed evidence, shared
> with the worker's consent, reaches an employer — as a
> requirement-by-requirement match report that keeps must-have, good-to-have and
> work evidence separate.
>
> The employer receives evidence about which workflows improve, what support
> people need, and whether results meet their standard: first-pass acceptance,
> total effort including review and rework, baseline against comparable
> reassessment, and consistency across participants.
>
> Built on AWS: Amazon Bedrock for assistance and rubric feedback, AWS Lambda
> for tools, Amazon EventBridge for job and profile events, Amazon DynamoDB for
> workflow state, Amazon S3 for artifacts, and Amazon SES with AWS End User
> Messaging Social for two-way coordination with candidates and employers.

### Next steps / delivery model

> Proposed: a two to four week employer pilot with 10 to 20 consenting
> participants and one approved workflow per function, with quality thresholds
> and a minimum worthwhile improvement agreed before the start. Expand where
> quality is maintained and the observed gain justifies the cost.

### Solution to associate

ACE requires at least one solution or AWS Marketplace product on the
opportunity, and only solutions in Limited or Public status can be attached. If
your organisation has no suitable listed solution, your Alliance Lead can point
you at the one to use — this is the second thing worth asking them in the same
message.

## Then

Copy the generated Opportunity ID into the Devpost field. Keep the ACE record
open in case the reviewers ask to see the campaign tag.
